How we choose our picks

We don’t pretend to test every tree ourselves. Instead, we comb through thousands of owner reviews on major retail sites, cat-behavior forums, and published recommendations from veterinarians and feline-behavior experts. We look for patterns: which models stay steady after a year of use, which ones lose their covering quickly, and which assembly instructions actually make sense. The things that break, wobbly bases, unraveling sisal, collapsing platforms, are surprisingly consistent across price points. So we zero in on materials: thick-walled tubes, reinforced screw holes, high-density foam padding, and sisal that’s tightly wound and glued. A tree that passes those checks tends to last, no matter the price tag.
